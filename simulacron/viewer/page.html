This is a recording, not a live simulation — the prototype runs in Python and this plays back what it did. Shading is believed danger, averaged across everyone's private beliefs, so a district can go dark without anything happening there: fear travels through the social graph faster than crime does. Patrol follows reported crime and nothing else.
